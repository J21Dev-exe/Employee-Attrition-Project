01 / OVERVIEW

Turn Workforce Data Into Actionable Intelligence

Workforce Intelligence

Workforce Intelligence helps organizations identify employee attrition risks before they become business problems.

By analyzing workforce patterns, employee engagement, compensation, experience, and work-life indicators, the platform transforms raw HR data into meaningful insights.

Why It Matters

  • Reduce unexpected employee turnover
  • Improve workforce planning
  • Identify retention risks early
  • Support data-driven HR decisions

02 / Prediction

How Attrition Prediction Works

Employee Inputs

Age, income, overtime, distance from home, job satisfaction, work-life balance, and workforce experience indicators.

Machine Learning Engine

An XGBoost-based predictive model processes employee attributes and identifies attrition-related patterns.

Risk Assessment

The system generates a probability score and classifies employees into Low, Medium, or High Attrition Risk.

Prediction Output

Every prediction includes a risk score, attrition probability, and business-friendly interpretation to help decision makers act faster.

03 / Workflow

How It Works

Functionality

  1. Enter employee information.
  2. Run the attrition analysis model.
  3. Review risk scores and predictions.
  4. Identify workforce retention risks.
  5. Support strategic HR decision making.

Business Outcome

The platform helps HR teams, business leaders, and decision makers understand why employees leave and where intervention is most needed.
